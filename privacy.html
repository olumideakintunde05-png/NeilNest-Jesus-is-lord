Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information NeilNest collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. NeilNest is a business directory and marketplace platform for buyers and sellers in Nigeria. By using NeilNest, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account infoName, email address, password (handled securely via Firebase Authentication)
Business infoBusiness name, category, description, location, logo/cover images, phone, WhatsApp, email, website, business hours
Payment verificationSender name, phone number, transfer date, proof-of-payment image, reference number — used only to confirm bank transfer payments
MessagesContent of messages sent through NeilNest's in-app chat between buyers and sellers
Usage dataPages visited, actions taken (e.g. follows, reviews, searches) to help us operate and improve the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your business listing to other users
  • To connect buyers and sellers through directory search and messaging
  • To verify subscription payments and activate paid plan features
  • To respond to support requests and enforce our Terms
  • To improve and maintain the platform

4. Who Can See Your Information

Your business's public details (name, category, description, location, images) are visible to anyone browsing NeilNest. Contact details — phone, WhatsApp, and email — are only shown to users on a paid plan, or to you as the business owner; free users see that these contact options exist but cannot view the actual details until they upgrade.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information with law enforcement or regulators where required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

NeilNest is built on Firebase (a Google Cloud service) for authentication and data storage, and Cloudinary for image hosting. Your data is stored and processed on their infrastructure under their respective security and privacy practices.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the platform's services. If you delete your account, we'll remove or anonymize your personal data, except where we're required to retain certain records by law (e.g. payment records).

8. Children's Privacy

NeilNest is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate NeilNest, including Firebase (Google) for authentication and database, and Cloudinary for image storage. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of NeilNest after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
